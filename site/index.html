What does Foreman actually manage?

OpenCode does the coding. Foreman saves workflow progress, checks required outputs, and manages which step runs next. Jev chooses between the allowed next steps. Small requests can bypass the workflow. Checks make the process inspectable; they don’t guarantee perfect code.
